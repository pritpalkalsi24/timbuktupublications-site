Author

Pritpal Kalsi

Pritpal Kalsi is an author, filmmaker and entrepreneur from New Delhi.

He began his career in marketing at Maruti Suzuki. In 1998 he joined network marketing, among its earliest pioneers in India, and went on to build his own company, moving more than 200 products, from electronics to consumer durables, through over five lakh distributors across the country. In 2005 he started a financial services firm in wealth management. Along the way he has travelled to more than 30 countries.

The stories never left. He made music videos in 2001, earned a diploma in filmmaking from the New York Film Academy in 2012, and went on to make short films.

Today he writes books that question what we’re told, and runs Timbuktu Studios, a home for film production and publishing.
