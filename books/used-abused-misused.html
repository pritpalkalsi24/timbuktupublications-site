From the author

Bhagat Singh has been my hero since childhood. I’ve read everything I could find about him, and about many great people since. With no one else have I felt a stronger connection.

At so young an age, he turned his energy into something extraordinary: a poet, a warrior, an activist, a philosopher, a citizen, a reformer against so many of society’s evils.

Everyone claims him. Few have truly met him. He is an eternal hero, and this book is my attempt to return him to his own words.
