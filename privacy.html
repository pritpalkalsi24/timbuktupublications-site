Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Timbuktu Studios Pvt Ltd collects through timbuktupublications.com and how we use it.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, email, phone number and delivery address.
  • When you subscribe: your email address.
  • When you write to us: the details you include in your message.
  • Basic, anonymous usage data about how the site is visited.

How we use it

To process and deliver orders, to send the emails you asked for, to answer your messages, and to improve the site. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the store: our payment processor (Razorpay), our delivery partners, and our email service. Each uses your data only to provide that service.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in each email, and you can ask us to see, correct or delete your information by writing to support@timbuktupublications.com.
